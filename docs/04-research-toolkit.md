# Research Toolkit — Steps 2 & 5 Evidence Collection

Use these instruments **before** finalising the strategy. Every claim in the deck should trace back to one of them.

---

## A. Competitor social audit (3 brands)

**Selection rule:** 2 *direct* competitors (Lebanese sanitary-ware or plumbing sellers with an active Instagram or Facebook page), plus 1 *indirect* competitor (a home-improvement or hardware retailer competing for the same renovation budget).

Candidate longlist to verify. These names come from public business directories, and their social activity is **not yet confirmed**. Replace them with any brand the owner considers a real competitor:
- Builders Sal (Bauchrieh, Metn): baths, showers, toilets, taps
- Arcom Group Sarl (Baabda / Sin El Fil / Verdun / Zahle): ceramics and sanitary ware
- Chromotech Sarl (Hadath / Sin El Fil): sanitary ware and plumbing
- A local sanitary shop that customers mention most often in interviews (ask the owner)

**Collection window:** the last 30 days of posts, gathered on a single day. Record only what is publicly visible. Do **not** estimate a competitor's reach or sales.

## B. Customer & plumber interviews (target: 6 to 8 homeowners, 4 plumbers or contractors)

Ask for verbal consent first, record no names, and keep each interview to about 10 minutes, in person at the store or by phone.

1. Last time you bought a bathroom or plumbing product, how did you choose where to buy it?
2. Did you look online first? Where exactly (Instagram, Facebook, Google, WhatsApp groups, TikTok)?
3. What would make you trust a store you found online enough to order without visiting?
4. What information do you need before buying a faucet or mirror? (dimensions, fittings, warranty, real photos, a video, price)
5. How would you prefer to pay and receive the order? (cash on delivery, card, Whish/OMT, pickup)
6. What time of day do you usually scroll social media? On which app?
7. Which pages or creators about home renovation do you follow?
8. *(Plumbers)* How do you get prices and check stock today? Would a WhatsApp price list or catalogue help you?
9. *(Shown twainyplast.com on a phone)* What would stop you ordering from this site right now?

Code the answers into themes: trust, information, price, delivery, payment, advice. Count how many times each theme comes up. These counts are what makes the personas data-driven.

## C. Mobile purchase walkthrough (usability test)

5 participants (2 team members don't count), each on their own phone, think-aloud, timed.

**Task:** *"Find a kitchen mixer under $35 and order it to be delivered to your home."*

## D. Persona validation survey (optional, Google Forms, 10 questions, n ≥ 40)

Distribute through the store counter QR code, the owner's WhatsApp contacts (with consent) and plumber contacts. Ask about age band, area, renovation in the last 12 months, platforms used daily, active hours, preferred content format (video / carousel / photo), purchase barriers (multi-select), preferred payment and delivery, and consent to a follow-up interview.

---

## E. Social listening (Weeks 1–4)

- Every day: log every comment and DM, with its topic (price, availability, delivery, technical, complaint, praise) and the response time.
- Every week: search Instagram and Facebook for `#حمامات #مطابخ #ترميم #lebanonrenovation #bathroomdesign` plus the competitor names, and note emerging questions and trends.
- Weekly output: the top 5 customer questions, which feed the next week's FAQ content.
