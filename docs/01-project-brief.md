**Project title:** *From Visitors to Customers: A Social Media and Digital Growth Strategy for Twainy Plast*

**Research question:** How can Twainy Plast use targeted social content, customer trust and an easier buying journey to turn website visitors into paying customers?

---

### 1. Client overview
Twainy Plast Sanitary is a family-run Lebanese retailer and wholesaler of sanitary ware and plumbing products, located in **[town / district — confirm]**. The catalogue has **175 products** across mixers and faucets, LED and decorative mirrors, WCs, vessel basins and vanities, showers, floor drains and domestic water pumps, priced mostly between **$5 and $250**. Customers are homeowners renovating bathrooms or kitchens, and plumbers or small contractors buying for projects.

### 2. Industry context
Building and renovation materials in Lebanon are still bought mainly in person, often on a plumber's recommendation. Shoppers want to see the product, check dimensions and fittings, and trust the seller on availability and after-sales service. Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp are where local shoppers find and ask about home products, but few sanitary-ware sellers explain their products or the buying process clearly online.

### 3. Core value proposition
*Twainy Plast helps homeowners and professionals choose suitable bathroom and plumbing products through clear information, practical advice and dependable local service.*

### 5. Digital gaps to investigate
1. **No structured social presence.** Social accounts are not yet run as a sales channel, with almost no social referral traffic.
2. **Weak purchase intent and trust.** Only 1.1% of sessions add to cart. Product photos are generated renders rather than real photos, some product titles are duplicated, and delivery and payment terms are not stated up front.
3. **Low local discoverability.** Product URLs are numeric (`/products/2-1`), search traffic is minimal, and the Google Business Profile has not been audited.
4. **No measurement set-up.** GA4 e-commerce events, UTM links and WhatsApp-assisted order tracking are not set up.

### 6. Proposed scope (Steps 1–5)
A competitor social audit (2 direct and 1 indirect), two validated personas, brand identity and channel architecture (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile, website), a 4-week campaign, **"Choose with Confidence — اختار بثقة"**, focused on in-stock mixers and LED mirrors, 3 Reels and 5 static posts or carousels, a **$200 test ad budget**, and a KPI framework covering reach, engagement rate, CTR, CAC, ROAS and conversions.
