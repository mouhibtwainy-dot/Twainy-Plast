## Reels to film yourself (storyboards in `png/reel-*.png`)

**General filming tips:**
- Shoot vertically (9:16) in daylight, with the phone held steady.
- Keep each shot 3–5 seconds.
- Record the voice-over in a quiet corner.
- Add subtitles and a trending sound from the Instagram library (keep the music low so your voice stays clear).
- Only show customers or their homes with their permission.
- Use **test details**, never a real customer's, when screen-recording checkout.
